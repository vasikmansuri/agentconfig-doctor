# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Posting real credentials, or exploit payloads aimed at people, in issues or pull requests.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability"), with a title that starts with "Conduct:". All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour violates this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
